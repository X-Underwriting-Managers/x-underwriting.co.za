Understanding gap cover

What Is Gap Cover?

South African medical aids pay specialists and hospitals at their own rate, often significantly less than what providers actually charge. The difference, your shortfall, is billed directly to you.

X Gap Cover pays towards that shortfall, protecting you from unexpected out-of-pocket costs when you need hospital care most.

01
Your medical scheme pays their rate
Your scheme settles the account at their agreed rate, which may be considerably below what your doctor or specialist actually charged.
02
A shortfall remains
The difference between the scheme rate and the actual charge becomes your liability. You are billed for this amount directly.
03
X Gap Cover steps in
Subject to policy limits, we pay the shortfall so you are not left with an unexpected bill after an already stressful medical event.

How the range works

Two Ways to Be Rated

The four plans divide into two families. The difference is not only what is covered, but how the premium is calculated and how every limit is applied.

Rated per life
Nexus 1 & Nexus 2

The new-generation options. A separate premium is charged for each insured person on the Schedule of Insurance, based on the age band that person actually falls into.

  • Nobody carries the cost of another age band. A younger member is never priced on an older member's rate.
  • No distinction between the principal member and an adult dependant. Each life is simply rated on its own.
  • Rand limits and claim counts apply per insured person per annum. A two-claim benefit is two claims each. The Medical Scheme Premium Waiver is the one exception, capped per policy.
  • The policyholder remains the sole contracting party and is liable for the total premium payable.
Nexus 1 · 14 benefits · From R113 p/m per adult life Nexus 2 · 19 benefits · From R130 p/m per adult life
Rated per policy
Vertex & Apex

Our traditional gap cover options. One premium is charged for the policy as a whole, on an individual or family rate, with the rating age determined by the eldest insured person.

  • The eldest life sets the age band for everybody on the policy.
  • Family cover is the principal member plus one adult dependant and up to three child dependants. Additional children may be added at R40 on Vertex and R50 on Apex; further adults cannot be added.
  • Rand limits and claim counts apply per beneficiary per policy. A four-claim benefit is four claims for the policy, not four each.
  • Child dependants are covered to age 21, or 27 for full-time students.
Vertex · 11 benefits · From R450 p/m Apex · 19 benefits · From R550 p/m
Why this matters when you compare limits. The same rand figure is not worth the same under each model. A R2 000 Hospital Account Shortfall benefit with two claims applies to the policy as a whole on Vertex and Apex, but to each insured person on Nexus 1 and Nexus 2. On a family of three, that is the difference between two claims and six. Always read a limit together with the unit it is stated in.
