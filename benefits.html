Two Plans, Comprehensive Cover

Both the Vertex and Apex plans are designed to protect you from the most common and costly medical shortfalls faced by South Africans with private medical aid.

The Apex plan includes every Vertex benefit, plus additional day-to-day cover, trauma counselling, robotic surgery, and enhanced value-added benefits. Use this page to see exactly what is included in each plan before speaking to a broker.
