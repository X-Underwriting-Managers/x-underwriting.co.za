The process

How to Submit a Claim

Most claims can be submitted by your broker through the Xcelerate platform. You may also submit directly to our claims team via email.

STEP 01

Receive Treatment

Undergo your in-hospital procedure, specialist visit, or covered treatment. Your medical scheme will process their portion and issue an Explanation of Benefits.

STEP 02

Gather Your Documents

Collect your medical aid Explanation of Benefits, the provider's original tax invoice, and your policy or membership details. See the checklist below for the full list of required documents.

STEP 03

Submit Your Claim

Email your claim documents to our claims team, or ask your broker to submit via the Xcelerate administration platform. Claims are acknowledged within 1 business day of receipt.

STEP 04

Receive Your Payment

Once assessed and approved, payment is made directly to you, subject to policy limits. Most claims are processed within 5 to 10 business days of receiving all required documents.

Required documentation

What You Will Need

Ensure you have all of the following ready before submitting your claim. Incomplete submissions may result in delays whilst we await outstanding documentation.

  • ✓
    Medical Aid Explanation of Benefits The statement from your medical scheme confirming what they paid and what shortfall remains. Request this directly from your scheme after treatment.
  • ✓
    Provider's Original Tax Invoice The original invoice from your doctor, specialist, or hospital, itemised with procedure codes, amounts charged, and provider details.
  • ✓
    South African ID or Passport A certified copy of the principal member's South African identity document or valid passport.
  • ✓
    Medical Aid Membership Details Your scheme membership card or membership number, confirming active membership at the time of treatment.
  • ✓
    Banking Details (first-time claimants) A cancelled cheque or recent bank statement confirming your banking details for payment processing.
  • ✓
    Completed Claim Form (where required) Certain benefits require a completed X Gap Cover claim form. Download it from the Documents page.
Important

Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the date of treatment. Late submissions may not be accepted. Please contact our claims team in advance if you have exceptional circumstances.

Common questions

Claims FAQ

Answers to the most frequently asked questions about submitting and tracking X Gap Cover claims.

How long does a claim take to process?
Most claims are processed within 5 to 10 business days from the date we receive all required documentation. Incomplete submissions may take longer, as we will contact you to request the outstanding documents.
What is an Explanation of Benefits?
An Explanation of Benefits is a statement from your medical aid scheme detailing your treatment claim, including what was charged, what the scheme paid at their rate, and what shortfall, if any, remains. You can request this from your medical scheme directly after receiving treatment.
Can I claim for out-of-hospital treatment?
Yes. Apex plan holders can claim for certain out-of-hospital expenses, including specialist consultations (up to R1 500, 4 claims per annum), primary care consultations (up to R500, 4 claims per annum), and preventative care screenings (up to R1 500, 2 claims per annum). Vertex plan holders are covered for in-hospital shortfalls only.
Is there a time limit for submitting a claim?
Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the date of treatment. We encourage you to submit as soon as you have your Explanation of Benefits and provider invoice. If you have exceptional circumstances, please contact our claims team before the deadline expires.
Can my broker submit a claim on my behalf?
Yes. Your accredited X Gap Cover broker can submit and track claims on your behalf through the Xcelerate administration platform. Many of our members prefer this route, as brokers are experienced in claims processing and can ensure all documentation is in order before submission.
What if my claim is declined?
If your claim is declined, you will receive written notification setting out the reason. You have the right to appeal the decision. You may also follow our Complaints Procedure, available on the Documents page. The FSCA Ombud is available as further recourse should internal resolution not be achieved.
Where is payment made, to me or to the provider?
Payment is made to you as the policyholder, not directly to the provider. You may then use the funds to settle any outstanding amounts owed to your doctor, specialist, or hospital. Your banking details are required for all first-time claims.
