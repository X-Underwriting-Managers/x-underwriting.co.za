Benefit overview

Four Plans, Two Rating Models

Nexus 1, Nexus 2, Vertex and Apex all protect you from the most common and costly medical shortfalls faced by South Africans with private medical aid. Every plan carries the same Overall Annual Limit of R223 000 and the same 500% in-hospital gap benefit.

Read every limit together with its unit. On Nexus 1 and Nexus 2, limits and claim counts apply per insured person unless the benefit states otherwise, so the same rand figure goes further on a family. On Vertex and Apex, they apply per beneficiary per policy.

How to read these tables
  • ✦ Benefits marked are not subject to the Overall Annual Limit. All other benefits are subject to the OAL of R223 000 from 1 April 2026.
  • Nexus 1 and Nexus 2 limits and claim counts are stated per insured person per annum. Vertex and Apex limits and claim counts are stated per beneficiary per policy per annum. The Medical Scheme Premium Waiver monthly cap is the one exception: it applies to the policy as a whole on all four plans.
  • Emergency Room illness claims are after hours only for beneficiaries older than 8. The higher per-claim figure applies to beneficiaries younger than 8.
  • Information is subject to change. Please refer to the policy wording for full benefit descriptions. The policy wording prevails in the event of any discrepancy.
