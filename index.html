The process

How Gap Cover Works

STEP 01

Your Medical Scheme Pays First

Your medical scheme covers the treatment according to their rates and benefits, paying their portion of the specialist or hospital account.

STEP 02

Providers May Charge Above Scheme Rates

When providers charge more than your scheme rates, you're left with a shortfall to pay, often thousands of rands out of your own pocket.

STEP 03

We Cover the Shortfall

Our gap cover pays towards the difference, subject to policy limits, protecting you from unexpected medical costs and giving you peace of mind.
